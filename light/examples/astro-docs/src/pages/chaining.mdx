## How chaining works

Yukibana runs a project: files and an entry. It has no notion of a
notebook's cells, so the playground makes one file: each continued
snippet's text, under a comment naming it, then its own. A snippet you
have edited contributes your edit; one you have not even scrolled to
contributes the text the page was built with. One consequence: an error
on line 3 of this snippet is reported at its line in the joined file.
