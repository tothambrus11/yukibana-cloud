## A snowflake in plain TypeScript

The page is three files: `index.html`, its stylesheet and `main.ts`.
You edit `main.ts`; the other two are under *Beside it*. TypeScript is
compiled in your browser by the JavaScript pack's runner, the page is
built into one document and shown in a sandboxed frame, and what it logs
appears under it.

Change `arms` to `8` while the page is shown.

## How it works

`Show page` asks the language for Yukibana's `preview` (from
`@yukibana/std`), whose session keeps one runner for the page, so the
compiler loads once however often you type. A rebuild waits for 300 ms
without an edit; a page that does not build leaves the last one shown,
and the errors are printed.
